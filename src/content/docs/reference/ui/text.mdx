## What is on screen

Every piece of text in the design is one of these.

- **`Label`** is one line of text in one setting. `type.screenTitle` and `type.heading` are two of the settings the design names. A setting is a font plus its line height and letter spacing, so you never type those numbers by hand.
- **`TrackedLabel`** is a label with extra space between the letters. The eyebrow at the top uses 0.16 em.
- **`MetaLine`** is the dotted line under a title. Plain parts get a dot between them. A `{ badge }` part is drawn in a box. An `{ accent }` part is drawn in the accent color.
- **`Paragraph`** wraps text at a width. Use it for anything that runs past one line.
- **`MarkedLine`** draws the `●` in a line as a circle, because the font pictures do not have that character.
- **`Badge`** is a small label in a box. `outline` sits on the ground. `onArt` is a dark plate for sitting on a picture. `live` is the red LIVE lockup with its dot.

None of these take focus. The counter under the picture shows what a screen of type costs: text and shapes share one GPU program, so it is a handful of draw calls at most.
