## What is on screen

Click the picture. Left and Right move along a row. Up and Down move between rows. Press OK on a chip to choose it.

- **`Button`** comes in three weights. `primary` is a white plate with dark text, for the one thing to do on a screen. `secondary` is a see-through plate with a thin line. `quiet` is just the line, for a footer. An `icon` adds a play triangle or a plus sign.
- **`Chip`** is a filter. The chosen chip is white with dark text. The others are a thin line. Choosing and focusing are two different things, so a ring can sit on a chip that is not chosen.
- **`Pill`** is a rounded choice, like a language in a menu.
- **`Bar`** holds a row of these. It does not scroll or clip, so the focus ring on the first item is never cut off. `bar.item(id, label, node)` adds one. `bar.strip()` hands back what the focus engine needs.

The three bars are in a **pack**, so Down goes from the buttons to the chips and lands on the one under where you were.
