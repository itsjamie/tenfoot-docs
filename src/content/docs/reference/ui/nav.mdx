## What is on screen

Click the picture. Up and Down move along the nav. Right jumps to the list. Left comes back. Press OK on a nav item to choose it.

- **`NavRail`** is the left nav. `items` are its labels and `selected` is the one with the accent plate and marker. Choosing and focusing are separate: you can run focus down the rail while Home stays the chosen section. `rail.select(i)` chooses one.
- **`List`** is a vertical run of rows. `list.row(id, label, node)` adds one. An episode list, a settings page, and a track menu are all lists.
- **`FocusRow`** is a row that lights up when it has focus. It is a normal `Row`, so it holds whatever you put in it: here a number, a title, a spacer that grows, and a badge.

The two strips are not in a pack, because they sit side by side. When focus reaches the right edge of the nav, the engine looks for the nearest item in that direction and finds the list.
