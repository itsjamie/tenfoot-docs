## What is on screen

- **`Poster`** is a tall picture with its caption under it. It can have a `title`, a `meta` line, a `badge` in the corner, or an `overlay` title drawn on the picture over a dark fade, which is how a genre tile looks. `blocked` says why the viewer cannot play it. The card turns gray and shows that reason instead of its meta line.
- **`LiveCard`** is a wide picture with its caption on it. `live: true` adds the red LIVE lockup. `progress` draws a bar along the bottom edge: red for live, accent for everything else. `corner` is a small badge in the top right. `tag` is a label along the top.
- **`ArtTile`** is just the picture, with rounded corners. It does not take focus. A hero or a backdrop is one of these.

The pictures here are gradients the page makes for itself, so nothing downloads. In a real app, the picture keys are file names, and the app's `resolve` function turns them into files to try.

The focus ring grows the card a little and draws a ring outside it. The ring follows the picture, not the caption, so a poster's ring stops at the bottom of its art.
