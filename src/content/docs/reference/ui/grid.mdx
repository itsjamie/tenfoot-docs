## What is on screen

Click the picture and press Down a few times. The first rows scroll up out of the way, and the row you are on comes into view.

- **`CardGrid`** is a field of cards with a fixed number of `columns`. `width` is the field's width, and `gap` is the space between cards in each direction. `grid.card(id, label, node)` adds a card, and the grid places it.
- The grid is the last thing on a screen, so it takes the room from where it sits to the bottom edge. Rows past that scroll. A grid that fits does not scroll and does not clip, so it costs no extra draw call.
- `grid.cells()` hands the focus engine a grid instead of a strip. Up and Down move by row and Left and Right by column. `rowFlow: true` lets Right at the end of a row continue onto the next.

This is the shape of a search results page, a genre page, and a saved list.
